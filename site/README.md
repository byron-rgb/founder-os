# Site and landing pages (Level 3)

Built from Damn Good Funnels (damngoodfunnels.com): Astro landing page templates and blocks, live on Cloudflare. Forms send to GoHighLevel out of the box; Claude can point them at another CRM.

Keys: the kit's own setup asks you to give Claude your GoHighLevel key. Don't. Say "help me with a key" instead: you put it in Cloudflare and `.env` yourself, and it never goes through the chat.

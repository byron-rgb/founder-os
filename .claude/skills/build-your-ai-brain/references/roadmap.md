# The roadmap → `Roadmap.md`

Write this after the read-back. It answers: "Now the AI knows my business, what should it do for me next?" It must be specific to them, built only from their brain, and useful even if they never pay anyone for help.

## How to build it

1. Read `4 Operations/Processes.md` (every `(repeat)` step), `7 Goals/Goals.md` (top five to hand off, the one thing this year), `4 Operations/Tools.md` and `6 Finance/Key Numbers.md`.
2. List every job AI could take off them. Score each one:
   - **Time back:** hours a month (estimate from what they told you, and say it's an estimate).
   - **Effort:** Easy (works today with just the brain) · Medium (connect one tool) · Bigger (several tools, or a custom build).
3. Sort them into the four levels below. In a Founder OS they map to its levels: 1 = Know, 2 = Connect and Build (Levels 2 and 3), 3 = Streamline (Level 4), 4 = Delegate (Level 5); name them that way. Put each job in ONE level only: the lowest level that can do it. Within each level, most time back first. The total counts each job once.

## The four levels

1. **Use the brain (works today).** Jobs that only need this folder: drafting proposals, replies to enquiries, content, quotes from their price list, SOPs written up from a voice note, prepping for a sales call.
2. **Connect your tools.** One dashboard that pulls in their real data (from the tools in `Tools.md`): money, sales pipeline, bookings, team. Name the exact tools.
3. **Run the business from it.** Their own system for the processes that eat time (from `Processes.md`): onboarding, bookings, events, team management, reporting.
4. **AI staff on a schedule.** Jobs that run by themselves every hour, day, week or month, within set rules: a Monday numbers summary, chasing overdue invoices, a weekly content draft, follow-ups on quiet leads.

## Format

```
# Roadmap for <Business name>

Written YYYY-MM-DD from your AI Brain. Hours are estimates.

## Start this week (works with your brain today)
1. <Job> · about <X> hrs/month back · <one line: what you'd say to the AI to do it>
2. ...
3. ...

## Level 1 · Use the brain
| Job | Time back | Effort |

## Level 2 · Connect your tools
| Job | Tools | Time back | Effort |

## Level 3 · Run the business from it
| System | Replaces | Time back | Effort |

## Level 4 · AI staff on a schedule
| Job | How often | Time back | Effort |

## Total
About <X> hours a month back if you did all of it.
```

"Start this week" must have exactly three Level 1 jobs, picked from their top five hand-offs where possible, each with the exact words to type.

## Closing line

End `Roadmap.md` with one quiet line, no sales pitch:

> Next: in a Founder OS, say "what's next" to start Level 2. Otherwise, want help with the next steps? Ask the person who gave you this skill.

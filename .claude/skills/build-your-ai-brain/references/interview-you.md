# Layer 1: You (about 25 minutes)

Four files in `1 You/`. One question at a time. Skip what the website or files already answered and confirm it in one line instead. The questions are a guide, not a script: follow what's interesting.

Open with: "First, you. The AI writes and decides better when it knows the person behind the business."

---

## 1. Profile → `1 You/Profile.md`

- What's your role, day to day? What do you actually spend your week doing?
- How did you end up running this business? (The short version. We'll get stories later.)
- What are you best at? What would your team or clients say you're best at?
- What do you avoid, or hand off whenever you can?
- How do you like information given to you? Short and blunt, or the full picture? Dot points, or a call?
- When are you at your best in the day? When should nothing be scheduled?
- Anything about how you work that a new assistant should know on day one?

**File sections:** Role · Background (3 lines) · Strengths · Hand-off list · How I like information · Working rhythm · Notes for anyone helping me

## 2. Values → `1 You/Values.md`

- What do you refuse to do in business, even if it would make money?
- What does "doing a good job" mean in your business?
- Think of a hard call you made recently. What made you decide the way you did?
- What gets someone hired, or let go, in your team?
- What do you want clients to say about you when you're not in the room?

Turn the answers into 4 to 7 plain rules ("We tell clients the truth about timelines, even when it costs us the job."). Read them back and let them edit.

**File sections:** Our rules (numbered, each with one line of why) · Things we won't do · What a good job looks like

## 3. Writing Rules → `1 You/Writing Rules.md`

These apply to everything the AI writes for them, on every channel.

- When you read something AI-written, what gives it away for you? What makes you cringe?
- Any words or phrases you never want used? Any you always use?
- Spelling: Australian, US or UK?
- Emojis, exclamation marks, dashes: love, hate, or depends?
- Long or short sentences? First name or full name when you sign off?
- Is there a writer, creator or brand whose style you like?
- Ask them to paste 2 or 3 things they wrote and liked (an email, a caption, a post). Pull patterns from those.

**File sections:** Always · Never (with a one-line reason each) · Spelling and punctuation · Sign-offs · Style I like · Quick test before anything goes out (3 to 5 yes/no checks)

## 4. Stories → `1 You/Stories.md`

Stories make marketing sound real. **Only write stories they actually tell you.** Never fill gaps.

- Why did you start the business? What was happening at the time?
- What's a turning point: a big win, a pivot, a near miss?
- Tell me about a client you helped that you're proud of. Before, what you did, after.
- What's a mistake you made that taught you something?
- Any personal story you'd happily share publicly? (Family, sport, travel, where you grew up.)

Aim for 3 to 6 stories. Keep their wording. For each, ask: "Happy for this to be used publicly, or keep it private?"

**File sections:** one heading per story · Story (5 to 10 lines in their words) · What it shows · Public or private

Close the layer: "That's you done. Next is the business: your customers, offers and how you sound."

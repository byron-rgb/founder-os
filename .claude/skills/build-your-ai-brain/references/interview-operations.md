# Layer 3: Operations (about 30 minutes)

Five files: `4 Operations/` (Processes, Tools), `5 Team/Team.md`, `6 Finance/Key Numbers.md` and `7 Goals/Goals.md`. This layer is what turns the brain from "knows us" into "can help run us". Push gently for specifics.

---

## 1. Team → `5 Team/Team.md`

- Who's on the team? For each: first name, role, what they own, full-time, part-time or contractor.
- Who do you go to for what? Who makes decisions when you're away?
- Any outside help: bookkeeper, accountant, VA, designer, lawyer, agency?
- Who should the AI never contact or write to directly?

First names and roles only. No phone numbers, addresses or pay rates.

**File sections:** Team table (Name · Role · Owns · Type) · Outside help · Who decides when I'm away · Do not contact

## 2. Processes → `4 Operations/Processes.md`

This is the most important section for the roadmap.

- Walk me through how a new client happens, from first contact to paid. Then what happens after?
  - Dig for each step: enquiry, reply, call or meeting, proposal or quote, follow-up, yes, onboarding, delivery, finish, invoice, getting paid, review or referral.
  - For each step: who does it, what tool, how long it takes.
- What other things repeat every week or month? (Content, reports, payroll, ordering, team meetings, bookkeeping.)
- Where do things fall through the cracks?
- Any written SOPs or checklists? Drop them in.

Write the client journey as a numbered list. Mark every step that's manual and repetitive with `(repeat)`. The roadmap uses these.

**File sections:** Client journey (numbered: Step · Who · Tool · Time) · Weekly and monthly routines · Where things fall through the cracks · Written SOPs (links to Sources)

## 3. Tools → `4 Operations/Tools.md`

Read out one group at a time, let them say which they use, and add anything they name.

- **Email and calendar:** Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar?
- **Clients and sales:** a CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive), a spreadsheet, or your head?
- **Money:** Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks? Payments through Stripe, Square, bank transfer?
- **Bookings and jobs:** Calendly, Acuity, Cliniko, Fresha, ServiceM8, Simpro?
- **Marketing:** Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Business Profile, ads, a newsletter tool? What's the website built on?
- **Team and tasks:** Slack, Teams, WhatsApp groups, Asana, Trello, Monday?
- **Files:** Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SharePoint?
- **AI:** which AI tools are you already paying for or using?

For each tool: used daily, sometimes, or barely. And: does it annoy you?

**File sections:** Tools table (Tool · What for · How often · Annoying?) · Paying for but barely using · AI tools already used

## 4. Key Numbers → `6 Finance/Key Numbers.md`

All optional. Ranges are fine. If they'd rather not, write "not shared".

- Roughly what does the business turn over a year? Rough profit margin?
- How many clients or jobs in a normal month?
- Average value of a client or job? How long does a client stay?
- How many enquiries a month, and how many become clients?
- Where does most of the money come from?
- What number do you check most often? What number would you love to see every Monday?
- Biggest costs?

**File sections:** Numbers table (Number · Value · As at) · The number I check most · What I'd love to see every Monday · Biggest costs

## 5. Goals → `7 Goals/Goals.md`

- Where do you want the business in 12 months? In 3 years?
- What's the one thing that would make the biggest difference this year?
- What's stopping it right now?
- If you got 10 hours a week back, where would they go?
- What admin do you hate the most? Give me your top five things you'd hand off tomorrow.
- What would have to be true for you to step away for two weeks?
- Anything personal you're working towards? (Health, family, travel.) Only if they want to share.

**File sections:** 12 months · 3 years · The one thing this year · What's in the way · Top five to hand off · Step-away test · Personal (optional)

Close the layer: "That's the hard bit done. Let me read it all back to you."

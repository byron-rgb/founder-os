# Layer 2: Business (about 30 minutes)

Four files: `2 Sales/` (Customers, Offers and Prices) and `3 Marketing/` (Brand Voice, Brand Look). One question at a time. Use what the website and files already told you: confirm, don't re-ask.

---

## 1. Customers → `2 Sales/Customers.md`

- Picture your best client ever. Who are they, what do they do, why were they great?
- What's going on in their life or business right before they come to you? What's the problem keeping them up?
- What have they usually tried before you? Why didn't it work?
- What finally makes them buy? What's the feeling: relief, ambition, fear, pride?
- What do they worry about or push back on before saying yes?
- Who's a client you'd never take again? What are the warning signs?
- Where do your best clients come from? (Referrals, Google, Instagram, LinkedIn, events, partners, ads.)
- What questions do people ask you over and over?
- What do happy clients say? Any exact words or reviews?

If they have more than one type of client, do the first five questions for each type.

**File sections:** Best client (per type) · The problem before they find us · What they've tried · Why they buy · Worries and objections · Not a fit (warning signs) · Where they come from · Common questions · In their words (quotes)

## 2. Offers and Prices → `2 Sales/Offers and Prices.md`

- Walk me through everything you sell, from the cheapest or smallest to the biggest.
- For each: what's it called, what's included, what's NOT included, and what does it cost? (Ranges are fine. Say if it includes GST.)
- How long does each one run, and what does the client walk away with?
- Which one makes you the most money? Which one do you want to sell more of?
- How do you price: fixed, hourly, packages, retainers, per head?
- How do people pay: deposit, instalments, upfront, invoice terms? What happens with late payers?
- Any guarantees, refunds or promises?
- Can the AI quote prices to people, or do you always want to see them first?
- What do you get asked for that you don't do?

**File sections:** Offer table (Name · What's included · Not included · Price · Length · Who it's for) · Most profitable / want to sell more · How we price · Payment terms · Guarantees and refund policy · Quoting rules for AI · Things we don't do

## 3. Brand Voice → `3 Marketing/Brand Voice.md`

Writing Rules (Layer 1) covers the person. This covers how the business sounds on each channel.

- Describe how the business sounds in three words.
- Which channels do you write on? (Email, Instagram, LinkedIn, website, text messages, proposals, newsletters.)
- For each channel they use:
  - How formal or casual? How long?
  - How do you usually open, and how do you finish?
  - Can you paste one real example you liked?
- Words or phrases that are "very us"? Ones that are "not us"?
- Is the business "I" or "we"?

**File sections:** In three words · I or we · Per channel (Tone · Length · Openings · Endings · Example) · Very us · Not us

## 4. Brand Look → `3 Marketing/Brand Look.md`

Keep this quick. Most owners have a brand guide or a designer: ask for it first.

- Got a brand guide, or files from your designer? Drop them in.
- Where's your logo? (A file, or a link.) Light and dark versions?
- Your brand colours? (Hex codes if you have them, otherwise describe them and check the website.)
- Fonts?
- What should photos and graphics feel like? Any examples you love, or hate?
- Anything the AI should never do visually?

Pull colours and fonts from the website if they don't know them, and mark `[CHECK]`. No website and no brand yet? Write "Not set yet", add it to Still to confirm, and note it as a Level 3 job (their site and pages set the look).

**File sections:** Logo files (where they live) · Colours (Hex · Use) · Fonts (Name · Use) · Photo and graphic style · Never do

Close the layer: "Business done. Last layer is how it runs: team, processes, tools and numbers. This is what lets the AI actually help day to day."

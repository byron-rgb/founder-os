# Quick wins

1. Read `quick-wins/Quick wins.md` and `Progress.md` (their level).
2. If they named one, do it. If not, offer three that fit their level and business (one line each) and let them pick.
3. Use only what's in their brain and what they give you. Missing a fact (a phone number, a price)? Ask for it, one question at a time.
4. Show the result. Make one round of changes if they want.
5. Save it in `brain/Work/<kind>/` with a clear name, add a line to `brain/AI Log.md`, and add it under "Quick wins done" in `Progress.md`.

Rules: drafts only, nothing sent or posted. No secrets in files. For contracts, say plainly it isn't legal advice.

# EA setup interview

> Driven Young Founder OS. Licensed to one business (see LICENSE.md).

Needs: email and calendar connected (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Outlook). Task app optional (Roma, Notion, others).
Runs: every weekday morning, on your computer (or the cloud).

Ask one question at a time, clickable options, recommendation first. Skip any the brain already answers. Look up their time zone from the brain or calendar; don't ask.

1. **What time should your brief be ready?** 6:30am, 7am (recommended), 8am.
2. **Weekdays only, or every day?** Weekdays (recommended), every day.
3. **Where should the brief go?** In Claude (recommended), a draft email to yourself, your task app (only if one is connected).
4. **Who always gets priority?** Read their recent sent mail and the brain's team, customer and supplier notes; suggest up to 8 names or domains (top clients, key suppliers, partners, accountant, team). They tick, add or remove.
5. **What should it ignore?** Suggest senders from their inbox they never open. They tick.
6. **Drafting:** draft replies to everyone who needs one (recommended), only priority people, or no drafts, just the brief.
7. **Anyone it should never draft to?** Optional (family, a lawyer, a sensitive client).
8. **Anything it should always flag?** Optional (refunds, complaints, the council, a big client's name).
9. **Tasks:** only if a task app is connected: include tasks due today? Yes (recommended), no.

Save to `brain/Agents/EA.md`:
```
# EA settings
Brief time: 7:00, weekdays (<their time zone>)
Brief goes to: Claude
Priority: ...
Ignore: ...
Drafting: everyone who needs a reply
Never draft to: ...
Always flag: ...
Tasks: no
Tools: Gmail, Google Calendar
Set up: <date>

## Your rules
(Every correction you give the EA lands here as one line, newest first. The EA follows these.)
```

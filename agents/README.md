# Agents (Level 5)

> Driven Young Founder OS. Licensed to one business (see LICENSE.md).

Ready-made agents. Each one is set up by a short interview, then runs on a schedule in the cloud. Drafts only: they never send, pay, post or delete.

| Agent | Say | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| EA | **set up my EA** | Morning brief of inbox and calendar, replies drafted | Weekday mornings |
| Sales follow-up | **set up my sales agent** | New enquiries and quiet leads, follow-ups drafted | Daily |
| Finance + bookkeeping | **set up my finance agent** | Money snapshot, invoices to chase, receipts missing | Weekly |
| Security | **set up my security agent** | The security check on a schedule | Weekly, deeper monthly |
| Your own | **make me an agent** | Any job you repeat | You choose |

Each folder: `AGENT.md` (the job) and `SETUP.md` (the interview). Your answers are saved in `brain/Agents/`. The `delegate` skill runs it all (type /delegate, or just say what you want).

# Sales agent setup interview

> Driven Young Founder OS. Licensed to one business (see LICENSE.md).

Needs: their CRM connected (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Attio) and their email (drafts go there). Calendar optional (calls). With no CRM: email only.
Runs: daily, on your computer (or the cloud).

Ask one question at a time, clickable options, recommendation first. Skip any the brain already answers. Look up their time zone and pipelines; don't ask what you can see.

1. **Where do your leads land?** The connected CRM (recommended), email only, both.
2. **Which pipelines matter?** List their pipelines from the CRM; they tick the ones to watch.
3. **When should it run?** 8am (recommended), 8am and 3pm.
4. **Weekdays only, or every day?** Weekdays (recommended), every day.
5. **How long before a lead counts as gone quiet?** 3 days (recommended), 5 days, 7 days.
6. **Where should the report go?** In Claude (recommended), a draft email to yourself, your task app.
7. **Anything a message must always include?** Optional: a booking link, a price page, a sign-off.
8. **Anyone it should never draft to?** Optional.

Save to `brain/Agents/Sales.md`:
```
# Sales agent settings
Runs: 8:00, weekdays (<their time zone>)
Leads from: GoHighLevel, pipelines "Residential", "Strata"; email
Gone quiet after: 3 days

Report goes to: Claude
Always include: ...
Never draft to: ...
Tools: GoHighLevel, Gmail, Google Calendar
Set up: <date>

## Your rules
(Every correction you give the sales agent lands here as one line, newest first. It follows these.)
```

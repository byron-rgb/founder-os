# Dashboard page library

> Driven Young Founder OS. Licensed to one business (see LICENSE.md).

Ready-made pages Claude offers while mapping a founder's dashboard. Each lists what it shows and where the numbers usually come from. Finance, Sales, Marketing, Operations and Goals are built into `dashboard-template.html`; copy their blocks from there. The rest are built from the blocks in `BLOCKS.md`.

Keep the sidebar to main sections (Finance, Marketing...), not one page per small thing. A section can have more than one page when the business needs it (for example Marketing and Ads under a Growth heading), like the Driven Young dashboard.

## Business

**Finance** (built in). Money in, money out, profit, real cash (bank + owed to you − bills). Money in and out by month. Owed to you, you owe. Spend by category.
Usually from: Xero, QuickBooks or MYOB.

**Cash flow.** Cash today, the next 4 to 8 weeks of money due in and bills due out, lowest point coming up.
Usually from: accounting (invoices and bills due), the bank feed.

**Jobs or projects.** Profit per job (table: job, revenue, costs, profit, margin), jobs running, jobs over budget.
Usually from: job management (ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO), accounting tracking categories.

## Growth

**Sales** (built in). Open deals, pipeline value, follow-ups due, won this period. Pipeline by stage. All open deals.
Usually from: the CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive).

**Marketing** (built in). Funnel from leads to sales with cost per step. Spend and return. Marketing spend by month and channel with a table.
Usually from: ads (Meta, Google), the CRM for leads and sales, accounting or card spend for tools.

**Ads.** Spend, cost per lead, cost per sale, return on ad spend by campaign; best and worst ads.
Usually from: Meta Ads, Google Ads.

**Social.** Followers, reach, best posts this period, posts scheduled.
Usually from: GHL Social Planner, Meta, YouTube.

**Website.** Visits, where visitors come from, top pages, enquiries from the site, searches people found you with.
Usually from: Google Search Console, Google Analytics, the site's form inbox.

**Reviews.** Average rating, new reviews, ones still needing a reply.
Usually from: Google Business Profile, the CRM.

## Customers

**Customers.** Active customers, new this period, repeat rate, top customers by revenue, who has gone quiet.
Usually from: accounting (top customers), the CRM, the booking system.

**Bookings.** Booked this week, gaps, no-shows, cancellations, rebook rate, busiest days.
Usually from: Fresha, Cliniko, Mindbody, Calendly, the CRM calendar.

**Shop.** Orders, sales, average order, best sellers, low stock.
Usually from: Shopify, Square.

## Running

**Operations** (built in). The week ahead from the calendar, tasks due.
Usually from: Google Calendar or Outlook, the task app.

**Team.** Hours this week, wages against revenue, who's on, leave coming up.
Usually from: Deputy, Employment Hero, Xero Payroll.

**Suppliers.** Bills due by supplier, spend by supplier this year, orders waiting.
Usually from: accounting (bills and contacts).

**Goals** (built in). The founder's goals from their brain, with progress bars where the goal is a number.
Usually from: `brain/` (no tool needed).

## Overview page

The first page. Four headline numbers the founder picks (one each from the sections they care about most), plus one or two short lists of what needs them today (follow-ups, late invoices, bookings to confirm). Keep it to one screen.

# Founder dashboard (Level 3)

One private page in your own Claude account that shows your business at a glance, live each time you open it. Headline numbers across the top (cash, money in, pipeline, what's next), then what needs you (follow-ups, bills, goals), then a section for each of these:

- **Finance:** cash in the bank, money in and out this month, owed to you, you owe (your accounting)
- **Sales:** open deals, what's in the pipeline, who needs a follow-up (your CRM)
- **Marketing:** new leads, website visits, social reach, where leads came from
- **Operations:** this week's calendar and tasks due
- **Goals:** your goals for the quarter, from your AI brain

Say **build my dashboard** and Claude sets it up with the tools you've connected. Say **update my dashboard** to change it.

`founder-dashboard.html` is the template (open it in a browser to see it with example data). Your own copy is saved as `my-dashboard.html`.

It only reads your tools. It can't send, change or pay anything.

# Your dashboard (Level 3)

Your own business dashboard: a sidebar of main sections (Finance, Sales, Marketing, Operations, Goals and anything else your business needs), each its own page, live from your tools every time you open it. It's a private page in your own Claude account.

Say **build my dashboard**. Claude asks what you want to track, one question at a time, maps it out with you, then builds it. Say **add a page to my dashboard** whenever you want to track something new.

It only reads your tools. It can't send, change or pay anything.

- `dashboard-template.html`: the template. Open it in a browser to see it with example data.
- `PAGES.md`: the pages you can pick from.
- `BLOCKS.md`: how it's built (for Claude).
- `my-dashboard.html`: your own copy, once it's built.

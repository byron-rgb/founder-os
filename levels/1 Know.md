# Level 1 · Know

Your AI brain. Claude knows your business.

## 1. Why
Generic answers come from AI that doesn't know you. Your brain is a folder of plain notes about you and the business: offers, prices, customers, voice, how things run, your goals. Claude reads it every time. In Obsidian you can see it as a graph and watch it grow.

## 2. Set up
1. Install **Obsidian** (free, obsidian.md).
2. In Obsidian: **Open folder as vault** and choose `Founder OS/brain`.
3. Before you start, drop your price list, any brochures or proposals into `brain/Drop files here`.
4. In Claude, say: **build my AI brain**. Claude reads your website and files, then interviews you across seven areas: You, Sales, Marketing, Operations, Team, Finance, Goals. Type or talk. About 2 hours, and you can stop any time ("continue my brain" picks it up).
5. In Obsidian, open **Graph view** to see it connect.

## 3. Make your own
Pick one quick win (say **give me a quick win**):
- Your email signature, built from your brain
- Your price list as a clean PDF

## 4. Check
- [ ] Obsidian open on the brain, graph shows
- [ ] All seven areas have notes
- [ ] Ask "what do we charge for <service>?" and Claude gets it right
- [ ] Monthly **brain check-up** in your calendar (add it yourself, or Claude does it after Level 2)
